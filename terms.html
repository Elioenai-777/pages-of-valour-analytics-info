Purpose and authorized use

Pages of Valour Analytics is a personal tool for the Pages of Valour channel owner. It is not offered as a public service. Only an authorized owner or operator of the channel may connect its Google account.

The tool reads channel data and creates analytics-report jobs. It does not edit or publish YouTube content. Any editorial changes are separate decisions made by the channel owner.

Reports and editorial decisions

YouTube data can be delayed, incomplete, revised or unavailable for small samples. Retention patterns and comparisons are aids to investigation; they do not establish why viewers behaved in a particular way. The owner remains responsible for reviewing conclusions and deciding whether to make changes.

Google services and data

Use is subject to applicable Google and YouTube terms, including the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Google API Services User Data Policy. The privacy page explains data access, storage, sharing and revocation.
