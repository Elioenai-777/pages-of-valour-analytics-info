Data handling

Privacy information

Updated 30 October 2026 · Applies to the personal Pages of Valour Analytics collector and this information website.

Information the collector accesses

With the channel owner’s consent, the tool reads channel identity, public and private video metadata, video statistics and aggregate YouTube Analytics reports. Metadata can include titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnail URLs, durations, publication dates and visibility. Reports can include views, watch time, engagement, retention, traffic sources, disclosed search terms, subscriber changes, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate.

Its Google permissions are youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or revenue reports. It does not collect individual viewers’ names or email addresses.

Purpose and sharing

The information is used to help the owner understand and improve the Pages of Valour channel. The collector does not sell data, provide advertising services or train AI models. At the owner’s direction, selected reports may be reviewed in an AI assistant; information included in that review is processed by the assistant’s provider under the owner’s chosen service terms and settings.

Google handles authentication and API requests. This public website contains no channel reports, OAuth credentials or access tokens.

Storage and retention

The Google password is entered only with Google. OAuth client details and access tokens are encrypted using Windows data protection for the owner’s account. Analytics files are stored locally. The latest snapshot is replaced on refresh, while daily reach reports are retained for comparisons and corrected when Google issues revised data.

API metadata is refreshed during collection. If the workflow is no longer used, the owner should delete stale API data within 30 days. Local reports, credentials and copies shared with other services can be deleted by the owner; deleting local files does not automatically remove copies already shared elsewhere.

Control and revocation

The owner may stop collection at any time and revoke the app’s access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access; existing local reports must be deleted separately.

This website

These are static information pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or application cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information to serve and secure the pages.

Contact

Questions about the collector or deletion of its data: pagesofvalour@gmail.com.
