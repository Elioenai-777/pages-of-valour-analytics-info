Pages of Valour Analytics

A local tool that helps the Pages of Valour channel owner understand how viewers discover and watch the channel’s films.

The collector retrieves channel and video information, performance reports, audience retention, traffic sources and thumbnail reach through Google’s official YouTube APIs. The owner can use these reports with an AI assistant to review titles, thumbnails, openings and storytelling.

Read-only access to YouTube content. The tool cannot upload, publish, delete or edit videos, titles, descriptions, comments or thumbnails.

How the connection works

The channel owner signs in directly with Google and grants two permissions: view the YouTube account and view YouTube Analytics reports. The collector checks that the authorized channel is Pages of Valour before saving the connection. It also creates daily analytics-report jobs for thumbnail impressions and click-through rate.

Credentials are encrypted for the owner’s Windows account. Reports are saved on the owner’s computer. This public information website does not connect to YouTube, accept sign-ins or display channel analytics.

Who it is for

This is a personal tool for the Pages of Valour channel owner, not a public analytics service. It is not a Google or YouTube product.
